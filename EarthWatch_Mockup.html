About this data

  • Source: NASA POWER (Prediction Of Worldwide Energy Resources), requested live from its public API. Weather values come from NASA's MERRA-2 model and solar values from NASA satellite products.
  • Place data: the daily point service, one request per place for all parameters. Yearly values are built from the daily values in local time.
  • Map data: the monthly regional service, one request per parameter. Indices that need daily data (heavy-rain days, wettest day, days with 50 mm or more) cannot be mapped this way.
  • Heavy-rain days: days above a fixed threshold, the 95th percentile of that place's own rainy days (1 mm or more) over the whole period.
  • Method: Sen's slope for the rate of change, the Mann-Kendall test for significance at 95%, without a correction for year-to-year dependence.
  • Limits: one grid square is far larger than a town, the values are modelled, and a trend is not a cause. This page shows the past, not a forecast.
